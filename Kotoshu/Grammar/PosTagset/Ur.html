Overview

The CLE Urdu POS Tagset as canonical data (issue #277).

Spec: "The CLE Urdu POS Tagset" (LREC 2014, https://aclanthology.org/L14-1250/) — 12 major categories with subdivisions, 32 tags, Penn Treebank + Common Tagset for Indian Languages lineage. CLE applied this tagset to the 100k words of the CLE Urdu Digest Corpus at 96.8% automatic accuracy; the human-reviewed purchase rides the same spec.

31 of the 32 tags are attested in the complimentary human-verified sample (pk-cle-urdu delivery, 2026-11-20). VB (bare verb) is recorded from the paper's schema and flagged SPEC_DERIVED until the full delivery attests it.
