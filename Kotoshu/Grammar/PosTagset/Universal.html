Overview

The Universal POS tagset (UD v2, https://universaldependencies.org/u/pos/) as canonical data for the Latin languages (issue #289). UD is CC BY-SA 4.0; the tagset names are the shared cross-language vocabulary used by every UD treebank (de/es/fr treebanks included), so per-language taggers and POS-keyed rules can share one reduced mapping.
