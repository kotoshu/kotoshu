Overview

Loads grammar rules from YAML configuration files.

Two formats load from the rules directory:

  • rules.yaml — the envelope format (=> [...]) consumed by the legacy Rule (conditions-based matchers)
  • *.yml — flat arrays of pattern-DSL rules (TODO.grammar/1), loaded as PatternRule; this is the format of the hand-authored rules and of LanguageTool-converted packs
