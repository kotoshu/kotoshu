Overview

Raised by backend selection when the native engine is explicitly requested (Configuration#backend = "native") but cannot serve the request: extension not built, or a dictionary type the native engine does not load. Failing loudly is deliberate — "native" exists for debugging and benchmarking, where a silent fallback to Ruby would produce misleading measurements.
